// ABOUT

🏫

NMSSVN College

Nadar Mahajana Sangam S. Vellaichamy Nadar College, Nagamalai, Madurai, is a reputed autonomous institution affiliated to Madurai Kamaraj University. Accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade and certified under ISO 9001:2015, the institution is known for academic excellence, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a learner-centric environment that nurtures innovation, discipline, and holistic development across disciplines.

💻

Department of Cloud Computing & Cyber Security

The department is dedicated to shaping the next generation of technology professionals. With a curriculum encompassing Cloud Computing, Network Security, Cryptography, Ethical Hacking, and Secure System Design, students are equipped with both theoretical foundations and hands-on expertise. Graduates pursue careers as Cloud Engineers, Cyber Security Analysts, Security Consultants, and more — ready to defend and drive the digital world.
